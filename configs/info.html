Running the bench

A complete 2G network with no radio hardware: Osmocom core, an emulated Calypso phone (QEMU for the ARM, c54x_exe for the TI DSP and its mask ROM), plus a 4G cell, a SIP softphone and a postmarketOS phone. Everything lives in /opt/GSM/osmo-operator[1]. Nothing telecom starts at boot: starting a network is your gesture[12].

Modes: one command per bench

All modes go through ./start.sh (start-native.sh is the same script, kept as a link). Add --regen to rebuild /etc/osmocom from the templates, --no-attach to stay out of tmux. Full option list: ./start.sh --help[2].

CommandWhat runsState
./start.sh --bts-classique --ms The default (red phone icon). Classic BTS: osmo-bts-trx talks to pont/pont_dsp.py; the emulated phone runs the osmocom-bb layer1 firmware and mobile, on the real DSP ROM[3]. LU, SMS, calls
./start.sh --bts-classique --ms-magnetite Same network, but the phone is a Motorola C139 running the real TI firmware (FreeCalypso Magnetite): its own L2/L3 and UI, colour screen in tools/osmo-c123-gtk.py --modele c139, traces by rvinterf[4]. Add --gapk for voice on the host. LU, SMS, calls in A5/0: ENCRYPTION="a5 0" ./start.sh --regen …
./start.sh --bts --ms --ts 2 The Calypso of QEMU is the BTS radio (firmware trx + host transceiver); a second Calypso is the phone and camps on it over the air (pont/air.py). --ts 2: one Calypso per timeslot (TS0 CCCH/SDCCH, TS1 TCH/F). camps, LU; no call yet (2nd TRX not frame-aligned)
./start.sh --bts --ms --ts 2/1TS0 and TS1 served by a single Calypso TRX (also the default of --bts).calls (audible on 20/10)
./start.sh --bts --ms --ts 1TS0 only (CCCH + SDCCH/4).LU, SMS, no call
./start.sh --bts --ms-magnetiteCalypso TRX + the C139 Magnetite over the air.experimental

--vocoder-rom / --gapkSpeech codec in the DSP mask ROM (default) or on the host (osmo-gapk / libgsm).--gapk keeps real time
--sap, --sap=pcscSIM over SAP: the card in the PC/SC reader (sysmoISIM) or the soft SIM[6].
--wan, --payload-ismLink several nodes (SS7 hub, WireGuard); SMS text over an ISM radio link[7].
banc-rssiThe osmocom-bb rssi app on a 4th Calypso (spectrum, sync, per-timeslot levels), fed by the pont in --bts-classique; banc-rssi --stop. Screen: c123-3.DL OK, UL view in progress

Safeguards

BTS#0 runs under a watchdog (BTS_GARDIEN=1, qosmo/run_modules/60-bts.sh): if osmo-bts-trx exits on “PC clock skew too high” it is restarted after 3 s. The pont caps the pause it asks of the BTS (PONT_CLCK_PAUSE_PLAFOND=38, pont/trx.py). Do not compile during a run: a loaded host still trips the clock.

Day to day

sudo tmux attach -t calypso          # the bench consoles
telnet 127.0.0.1 4254                # MSC VTY: show subscriber cache
telnet 127.0.0.1 4242                # BSC VTY
telnet 127.0.0.1 4247                # mobile VTY (osmocom phone; not with Magnetite)
./run.sh --logs   (in /opt/GSM/c54x_exe)   # DSP bench logs: /tmp/c54x-pont/*.log

Settings: globals.conf (MCC, MNC, ARFCN, A5…) or the settings page below; configs are generated into /etc/osmocom by --regen[10].
